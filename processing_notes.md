# Processing Notes — RDH in Encrypted Polygonal Faces (Tsai 2025)

- **Paper:** Yuan-Yu Tsai, IEEE TMM, vol. 27, 2025
- **Reproduction tier:** C
- **Status:** Completed (literature review + partial demo)

## Reproduction scope
**Reproduced:** vertex-index-ordering reversible embedding on a synthetic mesh (one bit/face, geometry preserved, exact recovery). **Not reproduced:** the paper's mesh encryption and results on real 3D models.

## Why full reproduction is not feasible here
No 3D model datasets or the paper's encryption scheme are available in the workspace.

## Honesty note
Demo numbers are from the included code on a synthetic mesh; paper figures are not independently reproduced.
